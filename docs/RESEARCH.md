# Product research

Research checked 19 October 2026 against public product documentation. No claims are made about private competitor ranking algorithms.

| Source                                                                                                              | Observation                                                                                            | Product decision                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Shaadi.com](https://www.shaadi.com/)                                                                               | Verification and structured matrimonial search are prominent.                                          | Show verification separately from journey stage; present full, grouped biodata.       |
| [Hinge preferences](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/360011063294-How-do-I-change-my-Preferences)            | Preferences can be flexible; family plans, children, and lifestyle are explicit preference categories. | Separate hard requirements from soft fit and treat uncertainty as a discussion point. |
| [Bumble filters](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/28423691289629-Using-filters-to-set-your-preferences) | Verification, languages, and interests inform filtering; adding filters reduces pool size.             | Explain eligibility and exclusion counts instead of silently broadening results.      |

Our additional fields include mother tongue, diet, smoking/drinking, marriage timeline, family living arrangement, career support, preferred cities, and declared values. These support practical conversations in the Indian matchmaking context. They are product proposals, not claims that all competitors use those exact fields or score them identically.
